Loss Surface

What you're seeing
The loss landscape around your trained checkpoint. Two random directions in weight space are chosen (filter-normalized per Li et al. 2018), and the loss is evaluated on a 51x51 grid. The surface shows whether the model landed in a wide flat valley (good generalization) or a sharp narrow pit (likely overfit).
