Training Diagnostic Categories

Which categories of diagnostics matter most for your training workflow? Select all that apply.

1

Convergence Diagnostics

What it answers: "Is the model converging? How fast? Is it stuck in a local optimum?"

Metrics: Log-likelihood trace, effective sample size (ESS), autocorrelation plots, Gelman-Rubin R-hat, mixing rate, burn-in detection, convergence speed estimation, chain diagnostics

Actionable output: "Burn-in too short — stability hasn't plateaued. Extend to 200 iterations." / "ESS is 12 — sampler isn't mixing. Reduce alpha."

2

Regime Quality Analytics

What it answers: "Are detected regimes meaningful? Well-separated? Tradeable?"

Metrics: Per-regime statistics (mean return, volatility, Sharpe, win rate, avg trade duration), silhouette scores, Calinski-Harabasz index, regime separation heatmap, regime overlap analysis, inter-regime transition quality, dwell time distributions

Actionable output: "Regimes 2 and 4 overlap 73% — effectively the same state. Reduce K_TRUNC." / "Regime 1 has 94% of bars — model isn't discriminating."

3

Feature Diagnostics

What it answers: "Which features are driving regime distinctions? Any dead features? Feature interactions?"

Metrics: SHAP importance per regime, feature correlation with regime assignments, feature contribution evolution over training, dead feature detection, feature redundancy analysis, per-regime feature distributions (violin plots)

Actionable output: "RSI_14 contributes <0.1% to any regime — drop it." / "ATR and Bollinger bandwidth are 0.94 correlated — keep one."

4

Parameter Sensitivity & Recommendations

What it answers: "How do hyperparameters affect outcomes? What should I adjust next?"

Metrics: Prior sensitivity analysis (alpha, gamma, kappa traces), hyperparameter impact heatmaps, stick-breaking weight evolution, parameter interaction effects, automated recommendation engine

Actionable output: "Alpha=1.0 produces 6 regimes, alpha=0.1 produces 3. Your data supports 3-4 distinct states." / "Kappa (stickiness) is too low — regimes flip every 2 bars."

5

Generalization & Overfitting Detection

What it answers: "Is the model overfitting to training data? Will it generalize?"

Metrics: Walk-forward OOS regime consistency, train vs validation metric divergence, regime stability across time windows, distribution shift detection, temporal coherence scores, cross-validation regime agreement

Actionable output: "Regime assignments in OOS window diverge 40% from training — model is overfitting to recent volatility regime." / "Regimes are stable across 5/6 walk-forward windows."

6

Run Comparison & History

What it answers: "How does this run compare to previous runs? Am I making progress?"

Metrics: Side-by-side metric comparison across runs, parameter diff between runs, regime quality trend over training history, best-run highlighting, regression detection

Actionable output: "Run #7 regressed on silhouette score (0.45 → 0.31) after increasing gibbs_iter. Previous alpha=0.5 performed better."
