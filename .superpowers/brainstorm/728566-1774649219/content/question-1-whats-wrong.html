Training Page Redesign

Before we design — what needs to change?

Here's what the current Training page looks like. Which of these pain points resonate?

A

Layout & Hierarchy

The page feels flat — controls, tabs, metrics, model browser all compete for attention. No clear visual flow from "configure" to "train" to "evaluate".

B

Configuration UX

Model picker is a bare <select>. No hyperparameter panel visible until you dig. No preview of what the model does before you commit to training.

C

Live Training Experience

During training, the convergence tab is a wall of charts. No at-a-glance status. Hard to tell if training is going well without reading every chart.

D

Results Presentation

Post-training overview is just a grid of "Awaiting Training" cards. No narrative — what worked, what didn't, what to try next. No comparison with previous runs.

E

Navigation & Discovery

Two-level tab nesting (ML Studio > Training > Overview/Convergence) is confusing. Model browser is a hidden modal. Hard to switch between trained models.

F

All of the above — full redesign

The whole page needs a rethink. New layout, new flow, new experience from model selection through training to evaluation.
